CLST Full-Height Turnstile

The CLST full-height turnstile provides the same operational functionality as our MST model, but adds a clear side panel and rounded edges — a less industrial appearance perfect for office environments.

The CLST comes in both manual and electric lock control versions, richly appointed with standard features that are expensive options on competitor products. It can be field-configured for one or two-way traffic flow with your access control system or push button. Card readers mount easily, and fail-safe or fail-lock configuration on power failure is simple.

In operation, when a user presents a valid credential, the turnstile unlocks, rotating in the requested direction. Unauthorized entries are not allowed and tailgating is deterred.

Key features
  • ✓ Clear side panel — less industrial appearance
  • ✓ Single or bi-directional traffic flow
  • ✓ Manual and electric lock versions
  • ✓ Built-in override controls
  • ✓ Free passage, controlled passage, or lockdown modes
  • ✓ Easy card-reader mounting
