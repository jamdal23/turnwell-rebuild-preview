MST Full-Height Turnstile

The MST is perhaps the most popular full-height turnstile in the field today. A proven design and solid construction will secure your entry for decades.

The MST provides one or two-way traffic flow in conjunction with your facility access control system, or any device that can provide a dry contact output (such as a push button). Card readers mount on either or both sides, and the controller can be configured to free-spin or lock in the event of a main power failure.

In operation, when a valid credential is presented, the turnstile unlocks, allowing a single controlled rotation in the requested direction. Built-in override controls add flexibility: passage directions can be set for free passage, controlled passage, or locked down.

Key features
  • ✓ 16-gauge steel construction for rigidity
  • ✓ Stainless steel top channel for added corrosion resistance
  • ✓ Sealed and covered bearings for smooth operation
  • ✓ Manual and electric lock control versions
  • ✓ Single or bi-directional traffic flow
  • ✓ Fail-safe or fail-lock on power failure
