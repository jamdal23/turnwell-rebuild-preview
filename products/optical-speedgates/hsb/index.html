HSB Half-Height Sensor Barriers

Sensor-controlled barriers with automatic swinging panels allow authorized, single, contactless passage in both directions. These time-tested sensor barriers are equipped with swing doors and offer many design options thanks to a variety of materials and finishes.

The stainless steel housing and transparent side walls create an aesthetically pleasing look. Units can be ordered in several lengths and barrier heights to suit your security needs — comfortable passage, even with luggage, and no contact with the door leaves.

Key features
  • ✓ 36” ADA-compliant width available
  • ✓ Easily integrates with access control
  • ✓ Multiple lengths for anti-tailgating needs
  • ✓ Modular — adding lanes is easy
  • ✓ Audio and visual alarm on unauthorized passage
  • ✓ Connects to fire alarm with dry contacts
