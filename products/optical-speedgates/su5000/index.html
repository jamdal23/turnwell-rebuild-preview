SU5000 Swinging-Barrier Optical Turnstile

The SU5000 with bi-directional motorized swinging barriers is the culmination of many years of optical turnstile design and manufacturing experience. It is simply a beautiful product: rounded lines, contoured edges, and liberal use of crystal-clear materials create an upscale look suitable for any premium lobby.

With the industry’s slimmest cabinet design, the SU5000’s compact footprint saves floor space and allows optical turnstiles where space is at a premium. To enter, users present a valid credential and the motorized barriers open away from them, allowing a single passage in the requested direction. If an unauthorized user attempts to tailgate — or enter without authorization — integrated sensors recognize the attempt and sound an alarm.

Key features
  • ✓ Bi-directional motorized swinging barriers
  • ✓ Industry’s slimmest cabinet design
  • ✓ Tailgating detection with audible alarm
  • ✓ Multiple operational modes
  • ✓ Integrates with facility access control
  • ✓ Crystal-clear barriers, premium finish
