SU2000 Barrier-Free Optical Turnstile

Sometimes you don't want a physical barrier — you want to know who's coming through and stop the ones who shouldn't be there. The SU2000 is a barrier-free optical turnstile: no arms, no glass, just a compact cabinet with sensors that track every passage and flag tailgating, wrong-way entry, and unauthorized presence.

It's designed for supervised entrances — places with a receptionist or guard nearby who can respond when the system raises an alarm. Think office lobbies with a front desk, museums, and corporate campuses where you want throughput to stay high and the entrance to stay open and welcoming.

The cabinet is compact enough for tight spots, standard and ADA lanes match, and the lid carries the same intuitive LED graphics as the rest of the SU family so users know exactly what to do.

Key features
  • ✓ No physical barrier — sensor-only detection keeps traffic flowing
  • ✓ Flags tailgating, wrong-way passage, and unauthorized presence
  • ✓ Built for supervised entrances with staff nearby
  • ✓ Compact cabinet fits tight lobbies and corridors
  • ✓ Standard and handicapped lanes share cabinet dimensions
  • ✓ LED user graphics embedded in the lid
  • ✓ Multiple cabinet finishes and lid colors
  • ✓ High throughput for busy entry points
