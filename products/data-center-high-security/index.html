Data Center & High Security

One tailgater through a lobby door can compromise an entire facility. We install the entrance systems that make that impossible.

Why Turnwell for data centers

Physical security your auditors can verify

Data centers live or die on physical security. The compliance frameworks your customers answer to — SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS — demand provable, auditable control of every entry point. Turnwell designs and installs entrance systems that enforce those policies without posting a guard at every door: secured revolving doors that admit exactly one authorized person per cycle, and personal interlocks that verify identity mid-passage with anti-piggybacking detection.

At Turnwell, we handle the complete installation — equipment, integration with your access control system, and commissioning. As a licensed General Contractor, we’re your one point of accountability from delivery to go-live.
