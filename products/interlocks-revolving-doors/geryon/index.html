Geryon Security Revolving Doors

Security revolving doors suit both interiors and entrances. Thanks to the high transparency of the glass elements and a wide variety of colors for the metal parts, all models blend elegantly into their surroundings.

A sophisticated sensor system — compliant with the latest standards — prevents user injury. Depending on security requirements, the door can be equipped with a contact mat, scales, or internal monitoring. Reinforced bullet- and burglar-resistant variants are certified to RC2 and WK3. Options include a rotating unit with emergency-exit function and night closure.

Key features
  • ✓ End-point locking — users cannot become stuck
  • ✓ Safety sensors per DIN EN 16005
  • ✓ RC2 and WK3 resistance classes available
  • ✓ All-glass units with underfloor drive
  • ✓ Emergency-exit and night-closure options
