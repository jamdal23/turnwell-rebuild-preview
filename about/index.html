About Us

Turnwell is a licensed General Contractor specializing in the installation of turnstiles, revolving doors, interlocks, and fencing.

Our story

Entry-control specialists, general contractors at heart

In 2013 we were fortunate to have the opportunity to open for business. Using our combined 40+ years of machine installation, service, and repair experience, we formed Mech-Elect LLC. Shortly after, we were awarded our first turnstile project from a major university in Virginia.

In 2019 we felt a rebranding was in order. The people are still the same — we simply chose a name that better describes what we do. Mech-Elect became Turnwell in 2021.

We are excited about the future and look forward to earning your business.
